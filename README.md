# Platformer_Panic
This was a part of my NEA coursework where I had to design a game for a stakeholder.
In order to run this you must have pygame-ce installed.
Windows: pip install pygame-ce
macOS: pip3 install pygame-ce
(make sure to uninstall pygame beforehand)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project's goal is to create a platformer game that informs players about different phobias and increasing awareness of different fears that many people have. The game will deliver an immersive, entertaining, and educational experience with engaging gameplay. Phobias are common mental health conditions that can significantly impact individuals' daily lives. Despite their prevalence, there is often limited understanding and empathy surrounding these fears. Each level will represent a specific phobia (e.g., acrophobia - fear of heights).

Player Sprite:
Inspiration: Deltarune / Undertale
I have picked these sprites as inspiration due to their minimalist, retro pixel-art style. 

Ability:
Inspiration: Thunder Shield (Sonic the Hedgehog)
My design will be similar to a normal shield from Sonic the Hedgehog.

Enemies:
Inspiration: Minecraft Spider
Even though spiders have eight legs, for simplicity’s sake I designed these spiders to have six legs. This will make sprite design and animation much easier whilst also acknowledging that these aren’t real spiders and just a virtual representation of them.
Inspiration: Tricky the Clown (Madness Combat)
I will be basing off my enemy clown designs on the original appearance of Tricky the Clown who has a simple yet unique design.
Inspiration: Balloon Boy (Five Nights At Freddy’s)
I will be mainly using his clothes as inspiration and will avoid including any features that can stimulate fear.

Leaderboard
Inspiration: Tekken 8
